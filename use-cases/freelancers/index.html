Your questions, answered.

How do email follow-ups work in DewCue?
Turn on email follow-ups for your webmail and DewCue adds tiny “Reply?”, “Follow up?”, and “Due 3 PM” tags to your inbox list, using only what’s visible on the page. You can also pick an email and set a time to return to it. At the scheduled time, the reminder prompts you to open the conversation and decide what happens next.
Create a useful email follow-up
Does DewCue automatically detect whether someone has replied?
No. DewCue does not monitor your mailbox or connect to your email account. The small “Reply?” and “Follow up?” tags in your inbox list are gentle prompts based on what’s visible on the page. You open the conversation, check for a reply, and decide whether another follow-up is needed.
Follow up without mailbox monitoring
What are buckets used for?
Buckets are a place for client or project update logs and notes. Use one to keep the context you will need when you return to a task. Bucket content stays local, and the Free plan includes three buckets.
Organize client follow-ups
Can I leave a sticky note on a web page?
Yes. Pin a note to any page and it reappears when you return to that page. Tap once to turn the note into a reminder. Free includes 10 sticky notes; Premium makes them unlimited. Sticky notes use the optional all-sites permission you turn on yourself.
Make reminders from web pages
What does Premium add?
Premium removes the published Free plan limits and adds “Can’t miss” mode (the chime repeats until you mark it done), full history, a calendar view, a water and medicine log with gentle streaks, unlimited page sticky notes, and the Sunrise, Sea, and Ink themes. You still check email replies yourself; Premium does not add mailbox monitoring or automatic email sending.
