DewCue for everyday life

A little help with the everyday things.

Small personal tasks can slip between work tabs: call someone back, remember rent, or make time for a break. Put the next action into DewCue while it is on your mind. Keep the reminders that matter easy to find, without creating an account just to start.

An everyday exampleType “pay rent on the 5th” or “water every hour 9 to 6 weekdays,” then check the saved schedule and choose how you want the reminder to appear.

Use your own everyday words

Describe the task, date, and time in plain English. A specific action makes it easier to know what to do when the reminder returns.

Give recurring tasks a place

Use repeating reminders for routines you want to revisit. Free includes five repeat reminders, with birthdays and a milestone included under separate plan limits.

Keep the nudges comfortable

Choose optional water, eyes, posture, or break reminders. Premium adds a water and medicine log with gentle streaks, and “Can’t miss” mode for the ones that matter. Use quiet hours when you want less interruption.

A little clarity before you start

Your questions, answered.

Do I need an account to create reminders?
No. You can create reminders without creating a DewCue account. Premium activation is separate: the licence server stores only the email address you enter and your licence key.
Read the getting-started guide
Can I create repeating reminders?
Yes. Repeating reminders are part of DewCue. The Free plan includes five repeat reminders, and Premium removes that Free plan limit. Check the saved repeat schedule before relying on it for a recurring task.
Compare plan limits
What can the wellbeing reminders help me remember?
Optional nudges can remind you about water, eyes, posture, and breaks. Choose the prompts that fit your day. The Free plan includes two wellness nudges, and Premium removes that Free plan limit.
Add a gentle break routine
What is “Can’t miss” mode?
“Can’t miss” mode is a Premium option for reminders that really matter: the soft chime repeats until you mark the reminder done. You can still set chime volume and quiet hours.
Compare Free and Premium
Will reminders arrive when Chrome is idle, closed, or my computer is asleep?
Reminder cards still appear after Chrome has been idle, and a “While you were away” summary shows anything you missed when you come back. DewCue can’t promise an alert while Chrome is closed, the computer is asleep, or the network is off. Test your actual setup, keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks, and review Today and Overdue when you return.
Test your notification setup
Can I keep birthdays and milestones in DewCue?
Yes. DewCue includes birthdays and milestones. Free allows five birthdays and one milestone; Premium removes those Free plan limits. These entries stay local to the computer you use. Check the available settings and saved details in your installed version before relying on them for an important date.
