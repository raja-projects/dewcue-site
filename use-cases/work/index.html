Your questions, answered.

How are my reminders organized?
DewCue separates reminders into Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Today helps you see what needs attention now, Upcoming shows what is ahead, and Overdue helps you revisit unfinished reminders. You can also search, add notes to a reminder, and pause a reminder or a whole bucket. A toolbar badge shows the count, and the side panel shows the same list.
See a simple work routine
What are buckets used for?
Buckets are a place for client or project update logs and notes. Use one to keep the context you will need when you return to a task. Bucket content stays local, and the Free plan includes three buckets.
Organize client follow-ups
How do email follow-ups work in DewCue?
Turn on email follow-ups for your webmail and DewCue adds tiny “Reply?”, “Follow up?”, and “Due 3 PM” tags to your inbox list, using only what’s visible on the page. You can also pick an email and set a time to return to it. At the scheduled time, the reminder prompts you to open the conversation and decide what happens next.
Create a useful email follow-up
Where will my reminders appear?
By default, a reminder card appears at the top right of the page you’re on, and it stays until you act. You can choose system notifications instead, or both. On-page cards use an optional permission for all sites that you turn on yourself. A test-notification button in settings, with a short fix guide, helps you check that alerts reach you.
Choose and check your alerts
Can I see my reminders beside the page I am working on?
Yes. DewCue’s Chrome side panel puts the same reminder list beside your current page with more room to view it. You do not need to create a separate set of reminders for the panel. Review Today, Upcoming, and Overdue to choose your next action.
