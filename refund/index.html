Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

A digital product

Dewcue Premium is a digital subscription. It is delivered instantly inside the Chrome extension. There is no physical product and no shipping.

Cancel any time

You can cancel a monthly or yearly subscription at any time. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not erase the reminders on your computer.

Refunds

Ask within 7 days of the first payment and we will refund that payment in full. A Lifetime purchase is refundable within 7 days of the payment. Later renewal charges follow the same 7-day window from the date of that charge.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They are processed within 5–7 working days. Your bank or card issuer may take longer to show the credit.

Write to hello@dewcue.com with the email you used at checkout. We reply within 1–2 business days.

The free trial

The 14-day Premium trial does not take a payment. There is nothing to refund if you never pay. When the trial ends, the free limits return unless you upgrade.
