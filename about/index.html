About DewCue

The ideaClear boundariesThe business

A small place for the next step.

A reply to check. A client detail to keep. A little water between two busy hours. DewCue brings those moments into your browser with natural-language reminders, email follow-ups, page sticky notes, project buckets and timers.

Today, Upcoming and Overdue help give reminders a place. On-page cards or system notifications bring them back when it is time. The side panel gives the same list a little more room.

Useful help, with clear boundaries.

You do not need a DewCue account for reminders. Email follow-ups do not need access to your email account or send messages for you. Reminders and selected preferences can sync through Chrome; buckets, notes, timers and history stay on your computer.

Premium licence records are separate. The licence server stores only the email you enter and your licence key. The privacy policy explains what is stored and where.

Who’s behind DewCue?

DewCue is a small, independent product made in India. The full published business details are available in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

Questions, ideas or something that slipped? Email hello@dewcue.com. The published support response time is 1–2 business days.
