What’s new in DewCue

Little helpers, right where you are.

Fresh ways to keep the next step in view, without leaving the page you’re on.

Cards that wait for you

Reminder cards appear at the top right of your page with Done, Snooze, Reschedule, Open page and Join for meeting links. They stay until you act, even after Chrome has been idle. Prefer system notifications? Choose those, or both.

Where reminders appear

While you were away

Stepped away for a while? A short summary shows the reminders you missed, so nothing slips quietly by.

About the summary

A slim on-page ribbon

The hummingbird ribbon shows a running timer or your next reminder. Drag it where you like, or hide it on any site.

Meet the ribbon

Sticky notes on any page

Pin a note to a page and it reappears when you come back. Turn it into a reminder in one tap. 10 notes on Free, unlimited on Premium.

How sticky notes work

Follow-ups on any webmail

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho and Hostinger webmail. Small “Reply?”, “Follow up?” and “Due 3 PM” tags appear in your inbox list, with no access to your email account.
