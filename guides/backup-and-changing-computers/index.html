DewCue backups and changing computers: what moves and what stays local

Understand Chrome sync, locally stored information, downloaded backups, and Premium activation before changing computers or uninstalling DewCue.

DewCue guides·4 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

The useful answer

Check synced reminders and local information separately before changing computers. Reminders, quiet hours, and the notification choice use Chrome sync; other DewCue records stay on the original computer, so download a backup and import it on the new one. Keep the old installation available until you have verified everything.

Identify where each part of your setup lives

Chrome sync is not a complete backup of DewCue. Use the same intended Chrome profile with sync enabled, then verify the synced records on the new computer. A reminder appearing there does not establish that your local notes or captured page information have moved.

Use DewCue’s backups

DewCue makes an automatic backup every day. You can also download a backup file at any time, import it on another computer, or export your reminders to CSV. The backup file belongs to you and is not uploaded by DewCue.

After importing, check your reminders and local information on the new computer before removing anything from the old one. Ask support if something looks different.

Verify the new computer while the old one is available

Move in a sequence that leaves you able to compare the two installations. Keep a separate record of important dates or notes if their transfer has not been confirmed.

  1. Review the reminders and local information on the original computer.
  2. Check the intended Chrome profile and sync settings on the new computer.
  3. Verify the reminders and synced settings that appear there.
  4. Download a backup on the original computer and import it on the new one.
  5. Check Premium activation separately and confirm your licence’s device allowance.

Distinguish removing Premium from uninstalling

Removing Premium from a computer inside DewCue unbinds that computer without deleting its reminders. Uninstalling the extension removes the local copy of your data. Those actions have different consequences.

Chrome sync may still hold reminders on your Google account until Chrome removes them, but you should not treat that as guaranteed recovery after uninstalling. A restored Premium licence also does not restore personal content: the licence server does not store it.

A few related questions

Is Chrome sync a complete backup of DewCue?
No. DewCue uses Chrome sync for reminders, quiet hours, and the notification choice. Buckets, notes, birthdays, milestones, and other local data are outside that set. Check both the synced and local parts of your setup before changing computers.
Compare sync, local data, and backups
Can I download a backup of my DewCue data?
Yes. DewCue makes an automatic backup every day, and you can download a backup file whenever you like or export your reminders to CSV. The backup file belongs to you and is not uploaded by DewCue.
Understand backups and local data
Can I restore a downloaded backup on a new computer?
Yes. Use import in DewCue’s backup settings to bring a downloaded backup into DewCue on the new computer. Check your reminders after the import, and keep the original computer and file available until you’re happy everything has arrived. Ask hello@dewcue.com if anything looks different.
Prepare a verified transfer
What should I check before moving DewCue to another computer?
Keep the original computer available, check that Chrome sync is enabled for the intended profile, and verify the reminders and synced settings on the new computer. Separately preserve the local information you need. Confirm the backup transfer process and your Premium device allowance before removing the old installation.
Follow the computer-change checklist
Why are my notes and buckets missing on another computer?
Buckets, update logs, and local notes stay on the computer where you create them. Their absence on another computer does not by itself mean reminder sync has failed. Check the original computer for that content and review the separate sync and local-storage lists.
Find the right place for your data
What happens to my data if I uninstall DewCue?
Uninstalling removes DewCue’s local copy. Chrome sync may still hold reminders on your Google account until Chrome removes them, but this is not a promise of recovery. Preserve important local information and confirm any backup recovery process before uninstalling. Removing Premium from a computer is a different action.
