How to check DewCue reminders and browser notifications

Understand the alert styles, use the built-in test button, and run a small check before relying on a reminder.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

The useful answer

Choose on-page cards, system notifications, or both, check the permission needed for that option, and use the test-notification button with Chrome open and your computer awake. Cards still appear after Chrome has been idle, but DewCue can’t promise alerts when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off. Keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks.

Check which alert you selected

On-page cards appear at the top right of your page and stay until you act. System notifications use the computer's notification display. You can also choose both. Start by checking the option you selected in DewCue so you know where to look.

On-page cards use an optional permission for all sites when you enable them. Displaying the card does not read the page. If you choose system notifications, check whether your operating system allows Chrome to show alerts. The test-notification button in settings comes with a short fix guide if something doesn’t appear.

Run one small, observable test

Use a low-stakes test instead of an important deadline. Keep the browser open, leave the computer awake, and note whether the alert appears where expected.

  1. Create a reminder a few minutes ahead and check its saved date and time.
  2. Confirm the selected alert style and any requested permission.
  3. Check your operating system's notification and do-not-disturb settings.
  4. Observe the test, then mark it done in your reminder list.

Know what a successful test tells you

A successful test confirms the conditions you just checked. It does not establish delivery with Chrome closed, the computer asleep, or different operating-system settings. DewCue does not promise every alarm will fire when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off.

Use a separate backup alarm for time-critical commitments. When you return to the computer, the “While you were away” summary shows anything you missed; also review Today and Overdue. If chimes are quiet, check the chime switch, volume, and quiet hours.

A few related questions

Where will my reminders appear?
By default, a reminder card appears at the top right of the page you’re on, and it stays until you act. You can choose system notifications instead, or both. On-page cards use an optional permission for all sites that you turn on yourself. A test-notification button in settings, with a short fix guide, helps you check that alerts reach you.
Choose and check your alerts
Will reminders arrive when Chrome is idle, closed, or my computer is asleep?
Reminder cards still appear after Chrome has been idle, and a “While you were away” summary shows anything you missed when you come back. DewCue can’t promise an alert while Chrome is closed, the computer is asleep, or the network is off. Test your actual setup, keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks, and review Today and Overdue when you return.
Test your notification setup
Can I quiet the wellbeing nudges during meetings or after work?
Yes. Set quiet hours for meetings or after work, and turn the soft chimes on or off or change their volume. Quiet-hour settings can sync through Chrome; the wellbeing nudge settings themselves stay on the computer you use.
Choose when nudges are useful
How are my reminders organized?
DewCue separates reminders into Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Today helps you see what needs attention now, Upcoming shows what is ahead, and Overdue helps you revisit unfinished reminders. You can also search, add notes to a reminder, and pause a reminder or a whole bucket. A toolbar badge shows the count, and the side panel shows the same list.
