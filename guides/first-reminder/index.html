How to set your first reminder in DewCue

Turn one thing you need to remember into a clear task, a sensible time, and an alert you have checked.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

In this guide

The useful answer

Open DewCue, describe the task in everyday English or tap a suggestion chip, and check its date and time. Choose on-page cards, system notifications, or both, then use the test-notification button. You do not need to create a DewCue account to begin creating reminders.

Start with one clear task

A useful reminder tells your future self what to do. “Call mom tomorrow at 6” is clearer than “Remember phone call.” Add enough context that you can act without having to reconstruct the reason.

  1. Open DewCue and enter the task, date, and time in everyday English.
  2. Check that the saved date and time match what you intended.
  3. Choose an alert style and create a short test reminder.

Choose an alert that fits your work

An on-page card appears at the top right of the page you’re on and waits until you act. A system notification uses your computer's notification display, and you can also use both. On-page cards use an optional permission for all sites when you enable them; drawing a reminder card does not read the page.

Use the test-notification button in settings to check your choice. If an alert does not appear, the built-in fix guide and the notification guide help you check your setup.

Give each reminder an honest outcome

Use Today for the tasks in front of you, Upcoming to see what is ahead, and Overdue to revisit unfinished work. The side panel gives you access to the same reminder list while browsing.

Mark a finished task done. Snooze it when you need more time, or reschedule it if it still needs attention. If you were away, the “While you were away” summary shows what you missed. Keep a separate backup alarm for anything time critical until you have tested delivery in your own setup.

A few related questions

Can I type a reminder in everyday English?
Yes. Type it the way you’d say it, such as “pay rent on the 5th” or “water every hour 9 to 6 weekdays,” or tap one of the suggestion chips. Check the saved schedule so it matches what you meant.
Write a clear reminder
Where will my reminders appear?
By default, a reminder card appears at the top right of the page you’re on, and it stays until you act. You can choose system notifications instead, or both. On-page cards use an optional permission for all sites that you turn on yourself. A test-notification button in settings, with a short fix guide, helps you check that alerts reach you.
Choose and check your alerts
How are my reminders organized?
DewCue separates reminders into Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Today helps you see what needs attention now, Upcoming shows what is ahead, and Overdue helps you revisit unfinished reminders. You can also search, add notes to a reminder, and pause a reminder or a whole bucket. A toolbar badge shows the count, and the side panel shows the same list.
See a simple work routine
What can I do when a reminder appears?
Each card offers Done, Snooze, Reschedule, and Open page, plus Join when the reminder has a meeting link. Mark it done when the task is finished, snooze it with one of your own snooze presets when you need more time, or reschedule it for a better moment. Choose the action that reflects the task's actual status to keep your list useful.
