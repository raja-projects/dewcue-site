How to activate DewCue Premium and find your licence email

Understand the emailed licence key or requested code, troubleshoot a missing activation email, and keep Premium access separate from reminder data.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

The useful answer

Use the licence key emailed to the address you provided, or the six-digit code if you requested one, to activate Premium in DewCue. Your Razorpay payment receipt is separate from the activation email. Keep your purchase email address available if you need help with the licence.

Find the activation email

Razorpay handles checkout and emails the payment receipt. DewCue’s licence key, or a six-digit code if you ask for one, is emailed to the address you give. A receipt and an activation message serve different purposes.

If the activation message is missing, check the address you supplied and your spam or junk folders. Avoid making another purchase just to troubleshoot an email problem; contact support so the existing purchase can be checked.

  1. Locate the licence email or the code you requested.
  2. Keep the email address used for the purchase available.
  3. Use the activation method offered in your installed DewCue version.
  4. Check that Premium access matches the plan you purchased.

Get help if activation does not complete

Email hello@dewcue.com if the licence message is missing or the extension does not recognize your paid access. Include the purchase email address, your extension version, and a short description of what happened. That gives support a useful starting point without making you guess at a different activation flow.

Exact button labels and code-expiry timing are not specified in the published information. Follow the available prompts in your installed version and ask support if they do not match the message you received.

Check paid access separately on another computer

The licence server stores only the email address you enter and your licence key. Reminder sync through Chrome does not activate Premium on a second computer by itself; enter your licence key there too. Confirm the computers included with your licence before adding another one.

You can remove Premium from a computer inside DewCue. This does not delete its reminders. It is different from uninstalling the extension, which removes local data.

Understand licence checks and your personal data

DewCue rechecks the licence about once a day. If it cannot get online, Premium continues for seven days from the last successful check. This grace period concerns paid access; it does not guarantee reminder delivery when Chrome or the computer is off.

The licence server does not store reminders, email subjects, or page content. Restoring your licence therefore does not restore those records. Review the backup and computer-change guide if you also need to move your personal information.

A few related questions

How do I get my Premium licence key or activation code?
A licence key is emailed to the address you provide. If you request a six-digit code, that code is emailed to you. Razorpay sends the payment receipt separately. Use the licence or requested code when activating Premium in DewCue, and keep the purchase email address available.
Prepare your Premium activation
I paid but cannot find my Premium activation email. What should I do?
Check the email address you supplied and look in spam or junk folders. A Razorpay receipt confirms the payment but is separate from the licence key or requested code. If the activation email is still missing, contact hello@dewcue.com with the email address used for the purchase and a brief description of the problem.
Resolve an activation email problem
How do I use my existing Premium purchase on another computer?
Keep your purchase email and licence key available, and enter them in DewCue’s Premium settings on the new computer. Confirm the computers included with your licence before adding another one; Chrome reminder sync does not activate Premium on its own.
Prepare Premium for a computer change
Can I remove Premium from a computer without deleting my reminders?
Yes. Removing Premium from a computer inside DewCue does not delete its reminders. This is different from uninstalling the extension, which removes the local copy of your data.
Understand removing Premium from a computer
Does restoring my Premium licence restore my reminder data?
No. DewCue’s licence server keeps only the email address and licence key used for Premium. It does not keep reminders, email subjects, or page content. Restoring paid access and recovering your content are separate tasks; use a DewCue backup for your content.
Separate activation from data recovery
Can Premium keep working if I lose internet access?
If the license check cannot connect, Premium continues for seven days from the last successful check. The extension normally rechecks about once a day. This license grace period is separate from reminder delivery and does not guarantee alerts when Chrome or your computer is off.
