How to build a simple focus and break routine

Choose one task, give it a clear time window, and make room for a break that suits your day.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

In this guide

The useful answer

Pick one task and use DewCue's stopwatch, a countdown with your own time, or the Pomodoro timer to give it a clear boundary. The ribbon keeps the timer in view and a card tells you when time’s up. Follow a focus session with a short break, and enable only the wellbeing nudges you find useful.

Choose a timer for the task

A stopwatch is useful when you want to see how long a task takes. A countdown with your own time gives a task a defined window. The Pomodoro option provides a simple starting point: 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5-minute break. When time’s up, a card lets you know.

Before starting, write down one achievable outcome. “Review the first two pages” is easier to act on than “Finish everything.”

  1. Choose one task and decide what progress would look like.
  2. Start the timer that fits the task.
  3. At the end, note your next step before taking a break.

Let reminders support your day

DewCue's optional wellbeing nudges cover water, eyes, posture, and breaks. Start with one or two prompts you are likely to use. You can leave the others off.

Use quiet hours or mute for meetings, uninterrupted work, or the end of your day. These controls apply to the wellbeing nudges; check your task-reminder settings separately.

Adjust the routine to fit the work

A timer is a boundary you choose, not a score you need to beat. If the task changes, choose a more practical next session. All timers, including custom countdowns, are included in Free.

Timer data and wellbeing nudges stay local, while quiet-hour settings can sync through Chrome. Check your setup on the computer you are using, and keep task deadlines in your reminder list so they remain easy to review.

A few related questions

Which timers are included?
DewCue includes a stopwatch, a countdown with any time you choose, and a Pomodoro timer. A card lets you know when time’s up, and the on-page ribbon can show a running timer while you work. All timers are included in Free.
Plan a focus session
What can the wellbeing reminders help me remember?
Optional nudges can remind you about water, eyes, posture, and breaks. Choose the prompts that fit your day. The Free plan includes two wellness nudges, and Premium removes that Free plan limit.
Add a gentle break routine
Can I quiet the wellbeing nudges during meetings or after work?
Yes. Set quiet hours for meetings or after work, and turn the soft chimes on or off or change their volume. Quiet-hour settings can sync through Chrome; the wellbeing nudge settings themselves stay on the computer you use.
Choose when nudges are useful
What syncs, and what stays on my computer?
Reminders, quiet hours, and your notification choice can sync between computers using the same Chrome profile with sync enabled. Buckets, notes, timer data, wellbeing nudges, birthdays, milestones, themes, follow-up preferences, and completed history stay local. Premium licence records are stored separately on DewCue’s licence server.
