What is DewCue?
DewCue is a Chrome extension for reminders, email follow-ups, page sticky notes, timers, and optional wellbeing nudges. Reminder cards appear right on the page you’re using, a slim ribbon keeps your next reminder or timer in view, and the side panel shows your Today, Upcoming, and Overdue lists while you work.
Do I need an account to create reminders?
No. You can create reminders without creating a DewCue account. Premium activation is separate: the licence server stores only the email address you enter and your licence key.
How do email follow-ups work in DewCue?
Turn on email follow-ups for your webmail and DewCue adds tiny “Reply?”, “Follow up?”, and “Due 3 PM” tags to your inbox list, using only what’s visible on the page. You can also pick an email and set a time to return to it. At the scheduled time, the reminder prompts you to open the conversation and decide what happens next.
What syncs, and what stays on my computer?
Reminders, quiet hours, and your notification choice can sync between computers using the same Chrome profile with sync enabled. Buckets, notes, timer data, wellbeing nudges, birthdays, milestones, themes, follow-up preferences, and completed history stay local. Premium licence records are stored separately on DewCue’s licence server.
Will reminders arrive when Chrome is idle, closed, or my computer is asleep?
Reminder cards still appear after Chrome has been idle, and a “While you were away” summary shows anything you missed when you come back. DewCue can’t promise an alert while Chrome is closed, the computer is asleep, or the network is off. Test your actual setup, keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks, and review Today and Overdue when you return.
When does the 14-day Premium trial start?
The trial starts the first time you exceed a Free plan limit on that computer. It lasts 14 days and does not require a card. Free plan limits resume when the trial ends unless you upgrade, and the Free plan stays available.
